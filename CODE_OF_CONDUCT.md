# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Tanikala contributors are expected to communicate with respect, assume good faith,
welcome constructive disagreement, and make participation safe for people of all
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized or
demeaning language, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, and sustained disruption of project work.

## Scope

This standard applies in Tanikala repositories, projects, discussions, review
threads, community spaces, and when representing Tanikala elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@tanikala.ph` with the subject
`[Conduct]`. Include relevant links or screenshots when safe to do so. Do not post
sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, limit participation,
or permanently remove access when behavior threatens the community or project.
Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with information shared only as
needed to assess and respond to the concern.
